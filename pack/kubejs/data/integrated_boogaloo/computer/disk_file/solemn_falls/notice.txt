TO NEW ARRIVALS

Welcome! The gate at the top of the hill checks your pockets, and it keeps what it checks. Bring one of each:
- something from the field (wheat)
- something from the smithy (an iron ingot)
- something from the trader (an emerald)
- something from a window (a glass pane)
Each one is in a gold chest somewhere in the village. Walk through with all four.

(added later) Please stop taking the glass out of the windows. You only need one.

(added later) If you're new and still down in the woods: climb a tree and get in the water at the top. It goes up. Only one of them comes out in the village, the others just hold you against the sky until you let go.

(added later) Do not go into the trees after the lights go down.

(added later) The sky is painted on. Don't touch it. It's warm and it gives a little.

(added later) whoever keeps standing in the trees, we can see you. we've always been able to see you. please stop smiling.

(added later) there are more of us than there were this morning and nobody arrived

(added later) WHEAT IRON EMERALD GLASS. WALK THROUGH. DON'T LOOK BACK AT THE TREES.
