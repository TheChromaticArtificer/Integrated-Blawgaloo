Crimson Gates are high-tier control parts used where Oritech needs to regulate large amounts of power. Their core is Antarchy's Blood Crystal, which carries energy the way Fluxite once did.

**Crafting**
- **Assembler**: Constructed from a [Processing Unit](@oritech:processing_unit), two [Blood Crystal Shards](@antarchy:blood_crystal_shard),
and [Titanium](@antarchy:titanium_ingot).

**Usage**
- Used in late mid-game and end-game blocks such as the [Particle Accelerator](@oritech:accelerator_controller),
[Atomic Forge](@oritech:atomic_forge_block), [Large Solar Panel](@oritech:big_solar_panel_block),
[Bio Generator](@oritech:bio_generator_block), [Superconductor](@oritech:superconductor),
and [Advanced Augmentation Station](@oritech:advanced_augment_station).
- Blood Crystal grows in Thoraxis. An [Enderic Laser](@oritech:laser_arm_block) harvests its clusters for extra shards.
